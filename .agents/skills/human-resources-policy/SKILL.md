## Human Resources Policy

### Leave & Time Off

| Policy | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Annual Leave** | 20 days per year, accrues monthly, max 5 days carried over |
| **Sick Leave** | Up to 10 paid days per year; doctor's note required after 3 consecutive days |
| **Parental Leave** | 12 weeks paid for primary caregiver, 4 weeks for secondary |
| **Bereavement Leave** | 5 days paid for immediate family, 2 days for extended family |
| **Personal Days** | 3 days per year, no questions asked |

### Benefits

- **Health Insurance:** Company covers 80% of premium for employee, 50% for dependents
- **Dental & Vision:** Fully covered up to plan limits
- **401(k):** 4% company match after 6 months tenure
- **Wellness Stipend:** $500/year for gym, mental health, or wellness programs
- **Remote Work Stipend:** $300 one-time setup for home office

### Code of Conduct

1. **Respect:** Treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with dignity and respect
2. **Non-Discrimination:** Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or bullying
3. **Confidentiality:** Protect proprietary information and personal data
4. **Conflict of Interest:** Disclose any personal or financial conflicts promptly
5. **Ethical Conduct:** Report violations through the anonymous ethics hotline

### Performance

- **Reviews:** Conducted semi-annually (June and December)
- **Probation Period:** 90 days for new hires, extendable once by 30 days
- **PIP Duration:** Typically 30–60 days with weekly check-ins
- **Promotion Cycle:** Annual, reviewed in Q4 for Q1 effective dates

### Remote & Hybrid Work

- **Hybrid Schedule:** Minimum 2 days in-office per week (Tue & Thu recommended)
- **Remote Eligibility:** After 6 months tenure, with manager approval
- **Core Hours:** 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time for collaboration
- **Time Zones:** Flexible outside core hours; respect async communication norms

### Expense Reimbursement

| Category | Limit | Approval Required |
|----------|-------|-------------------|
| Meals (client) | $75/person | Manager |
| Travel (domestic) | $2,000/trip | Director |
| Travel (international) | $5,000/trip | VP |
| Equipment | $1,500 | Manager |
| Professional Development | $2,000/year | HR |

### How to Use This Policy

When answering HR questions:
1. Check the relevant section above for the answer
2. If the policy doesn't cover the specific scenario, advise the user to contact HR directly at hr@company.com
3. Never make up policies — only reference what is documented here
4. For urgent matters (harassment, safety), always escalate to HR or a manager immediately
